What we do?

As an NGO, we address period poverty through education, access and acceptance.

Education & Awareness

We educate women and girls about menstrual health, hygiene and safe product use.

Access to Products

We guide women and girls to affordable menstrual products and provide free or subsidised supplies.

Encouraging Acceptance

We engage families, boys and men to challenge myths and normalise conversations about periods.
